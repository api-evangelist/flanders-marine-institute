# Flanders Marine Institute

The Flanders Marine Institute (Vlaams Instituut voor de Zee, VLIZ) is the Belgian coordination and information platform for marine research, and the operator of Marine Regions, the World Register of Marine Species gazetteer infrastructure and the EMODnet data portals. Its geospatial estate is one of the largest openly callable marine OGC surfaces in Europe.

Profiled 2026-08-20 as part of the [OGC](../ogc/) standards-body pass — this organization is an
**OGC Explorer member** (Non-Profit, Belgium), found in OGC's own
active-member roster and confirmed to serve a live OGC surface on its own host.
